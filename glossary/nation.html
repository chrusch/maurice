A Glossary of Maurice’s Terms

nation

national · national life

Frequency in the corpus: nation and nations 1,608; national 432.

In brief. A divine order of human society, standing between the family and the universal Church. Maurice reads the Bible as moving from family life to national life to the life of mankind, with God revealed at each stage. National life is holy, not a worldly accident to be outgrown, and the Church exists to teach and bless nations, never to replace them. Israel is the pattern and the abiding sign of this.

Shades of meaning

  1. The divinity of national life. Maurice thinks the Manichean contempt for the social order will never be overcome “till we acknowledge the sanctity, the grandeur, the divinity of national life” (The Kingdom of Christ II, “On the Relations of the Church with National Bodies”). “See whether every page of the Old Testament is not setting forth to us the glory of national life” (The Gospel of the Kingdom of Heaven, Lecture XXIII).

  2. A stage of revelation. Scripture begins with the family, “Does it not go on to the facts of National life, discovering an I Am, an unchangeable Lawgiver and King and Judge in the midst of them?” (What is Revelation?, Letter VI). The name of the I Am “was the ground of the National polity” before it was expanded into the Name of the Trinity, “the foundation of the universal Family” (The Unity of the New Testament, “St. Matthew”). See Trinity.

  3. Israel as sign. “I look upon the Jewish nation as an abiding sign to the Christian Church of the honour which God has put upon national life, and of His will that the Church should never strive to set itself up as something separate from the nations” (The Kingdom of Christ II, “The Modern Interpreters of Prophecy”). Israel existed “to be a witness for this universal fellowship among the nations” (What is Revelation?, Sermon III, “St. Paul at Athens”). See sign.

  4. A unity to be kept. Like a family, a nation has a given unity that strife presupposes: “There is a Family Unity; a Unity to be kept, not created … It is so with every nation, however torn in pieces by old class hostilities or by new revolutions” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “The Bond of Peace”). Maurice speaks of “the nation as an imperishable substance, when all its outward accidents were changed” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “The Sealed Nation and the Universal Family”).

  5. The Church as teacher, not rival. The Church’s failure was that it “has set itself up as a rival to the nations of the earth, instead of being their teacher” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel IV, “The Uniting Name”). A national Church “exists to protest against these outrages upon that which is the very ground of a nation’s existence” (The Kingdom of Christ II, “The Modern Statesman”). See Church.

  6. A vocation. Nations exist for a purpose: “every man and every nation exists for the purpose of chasing falsehood and evil out of God’s universe” (The Religions of the World, Lecture I).

  7. National judgment. Because nations are under God, their calamities are his discipline: “in every national judgment, in every individual trial, the Refiner is sitting to destroy the dross, to bring forth the pure ore” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel V, “Christ the Purifier of Priests and Laymen”).

Neighbouring words

  • family (the order before it). Senses 2 and 4.
  • universal family, Church, kingdom (the order beyond it). Senses 2 and 5; see kingdom.
  • Israel, the Jewish nation (its pattern). Sense 3.
  • I Am, Lawgiver, King, Judge (God as revealed in it). Sense 2.
  • class, sect (its dividers). Sense 4.
  • national Church (its witness). Sense 5.
  • judgment (its discipline). Sense 7.

Scripture. Genesis 12:3, in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed, behind sense 2; Exodus 19:6, a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation; Acts 17:26, hath made of one blood all nations of men … and hath determined … the bounds of their habitation, the text of “St. Paul at Athens”; Matthew 28:19, teach all nations; Malachi 3:3, he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver (sense 7).
