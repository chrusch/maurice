A Glossary of Maurice’s Terms

Spirit

Holy Ghost · Comforter

Frequency in the corpus: spirit 2,980 (the Spirit 1,158); Holy Ghost 142; Holy Spirit 101; Comforter 50.

In brief. The third Person of the Trinity, a living and personal presence, not an influence. Maurice insists on personality: the Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son and is the bond of their union, and he dwells among men now. He is the source of every human gift, of healing, speech and government, and the power who unites men in one Church. He is also the Spirit of truth, set against the spirits of lies and party.

Shades of meaning

  1. A person, not an influence. “The Spirit of God is dwelling amongst us, is dwelling in us. The rain and the dew are apt symbols of His influences; but they do not denote that Living and Personal Presence, that abiding Friend and Comforter Who proceeds from the Father and the Son” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “Universality of the Whitsuntide Message”). On the promises of the Comforter: “All here is personal in the strictest sense” (Theological Essays, “On the Personality and Teaching of the Holy Spirit”). A vague faith in “Influences or a pervading universal Spirit” empties God of his character (The Doctrine of Sacrifice, Dedicatory Letter).

  2. Bond of the Father and the Son. The same Whitsunday sermon calls him the one “Who is the bond of their unalterable union” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “Universality of the Whitsuntide Message”). He is how men know them: “You cannot know the Father and the Son, unless the Spirit of the Father and the Son be working in you, working in you to transform your will to His Will” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “Conformity to the Mind of God”).

  3. Giver of all gifts. Every power in men comes from him: “all powers of healing, of speech, of government, by whomsoever they are exercised, proceed from the Spirit of God, and are the signs of His presence with men” (The Epistles of St. John, Lecture X). The Gospel is “that all gifts with which men are endowed are gifts of the Holy Spirit, and are signs of His presence” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “A Straitened Faith and a Narrow Gospel Not the Strait Gate and the Narrow Way of Christ”).

  4. Maker of the Church. In the Creed he comes first and the Church depends on him: the Creed speaks of “a Holy Spirit who has established a holy universal Church, who makes men a communion of saints” (The Kingdom of Christ II, “The Creeds”). To reverse the order is the error of churches that “have made the Spirit dependent upon the body which He quickens; not the body upon the Spirit” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “Universality of the Whitsuntide Message”).

  5. Spirit of unity. God “has given us His own spirit, that we may be delivered from all partial and narrow notions which separate us from one another” (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “The Fatherly Character of God the Only Ground of Repentance, Consolation, Hope”).

  6. Spirit of truth. He is set against other spirits. “To proclaim the Spirit of Truth against these spirits of lies” is the Church’s work (Sermons Preached in Lincoln’s Inn Chapel II, “Universality of the Whitsuntide Message”). “The baptism with the Spirit of truth is the deliverance from the spirit of hypocrisy” (The Gospel of the Kingdom of Heaven, Lecture XVI).

  7. The spirit of an age or a world. In lower case, spirit is a temper or a power that rules a society; believers are to be “conquerors of the world’s Spirit” (The Epistles of St. John, Lecture XVI). See world.

Neighbouring words

  • Father, Son (those he unites). Sense 2; see Father.
  • influence (the impersonal notion he rejects). Sense 1.
  • Comforter, Friend, Teacher (personal titles). Sense 1.
  • gifts, powers (his signs). Sense 3; see sign.
  • Church, communion of saints (his work). Sense 4; see Church.
  • baptism (his sacrament). Sense 6; see baptism.
  • world, spirit of the age (the rival spirit). Sense 7.

Scripture. John 15:26, the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father; John 16:8, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment, expounded in the essay on the Holy Spirit; 1 Corinthians 12:4, there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit (sense 3); Psalm 68:18, that the Lord God might dwell among them, quoted in the Whitsunday sermon; Acts 2.
