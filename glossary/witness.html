A Glossary of Maurice’s Terms

witness

testimony · bear witness

Frequency in the corpus: witness and witnesses 901, with testimony and testify.

In brief. The calling of every man, nation and Church: to testify of a God who is present and of a truth that already is. Christ is the faithful and true Witness; Israel was called out to bear witness for a living God among idolaters; the Church is a witness to the world of facts that the world naturally denies. Maurice’s habit of reading sects and systems for what they witness to belongs here: each bears witness to some truth, even while it distorts it. A witness that ceases to testify loses its reason for existing.

Shades of meaning

  1. Christ the true Witness. The first title in the Apocalypse is “That He is the faithful and true Witness of the Eternal God” (Lectures on the Apocalypse, Lecture I). See revelation.

  2. Israel called to witness. “Israel, not a few picked men out of it, but the whole nation—the twelve tribes—had been called out to bear witness for a living God” (Lectures on the Apocalypse, Lecture VII). Each nation may be “sealed and set apart by the living God as a witness for Him” (same). See nation.

  3. The Church a witness against the world. The Church is “especially the witness for these facts which it is natural to us to deny” (The Kingdom of Christ I, “Signs of a Spiritual Society”), namely men’s relation to God and their common humanity. It must therefore stay distinct: “In losing its distinctness it loses its meaning” (same). It is “a Church bearing witness of the Atonement to mankind” (The Doctrine of Sacrifice, Sermon XIII). See Church and world.

  4. A witness that fails. A witness exists to testify, and can cease to be one. Jerusalem came to a point where “it would have been a lie for Jerusalem any longer to pretend that it was a witness for God” (Lectures on the Apocalypse, Lecture IX). Israel “were there to be witnesses of a truth—to do a work. If they were not witnesses of this truth, if they failed to do this work” the land was lost (Sermons Preached in Country Churches, X, “The Law of Inheritance”).

  5. Witness in parties and systems. Maurice reads opponents for their positive testimony: “I could see that the Romanist was bearing witness for an Universal Church, governed and filled by the Spirit of God,—though I thought that he had enfeebled and destroyed that witness” (The Doctrine of Sacrifice, Dedicatory Letter). The rival systems “each to bear witness of the existence of a Divine Order” (The Kingdom of Christ II, “Modern English Systems”). See system, sense 3.

  6. Witnesses in conscience and nature. “Every sin of which our heart condemns us, is a witness of a righteous Lord who is the enemy of sin” (Sermons Preached in Country Churches, IV, “Waiting for Christ”). The heavens “bear witness of Him; see how they tell of a living and unchangeable God” (Sermons Preached in Country Churches, XVI, “Man’s Dominion”). See conscience.

  7. Signs as witnesses. Institutions and acts witness to realities: the tabernacle “was the witness of God’s abiding presence with the people” (The Doctrine of Sacrifice, Sermon V); “The blood of the Lamb was a perpetual witness to them that God had reconciled the world unto Himself” (Lectures on the Apocalypse, Lecture XII). See sign and sacrament.

Neighbouring words

  • sign, sacrament, pledge (witnesses in act). Sense 7.
  • Church, nation (the witnessing bodies). Senses 2 and 3.
  • world (what the Church witnesses against). Sense 3; see world.
  • conscience (the inward witness). Sense 6.
  • prophet, martyr (personal witnesses). Elijah and the prophets bear witness against Baal and every false worship.
  • idolatry (what witness is against). Senses 2 and 3.
  • calling, election (its ground). A people is chosen to witness, not to be favoured.

Scripture. Revelation 1:5 and 3:14, the faithful and true witness (sense 1); Isaiah 43:10, Ye are my witnesses, saith the LORD (sense 2); Acts 1:8, ye shall be witnesses unto me … unto the uttermost part of the earth (sense 3); Acts 5:32, we are his witnesses of these things; and so is also the Holy Ghost; Revelation 11:3, my two witnesses.
