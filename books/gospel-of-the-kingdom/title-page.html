THE GOSPEL

A Course of Lectures on the Gospel of St. Luke

BY

FREDERICK DENISON MAURICE

‘Come forth out of Thy royall chambers, O Prince of all the kings of the earth! Put on the robes of Thy imperiall majesty! take up that unlimited sceptre which Thy Almighty Father hath bequeath’d Thee! for now the voice of Thy Bride calls Thee, and all creatures sigh to bee renew’d.’—Milton.
